## Masking

Both passwords are registered as secrets at start-up; wherever they would appear (step names, logs, typed values,
errors, dashboard) they show as `••••••`.

## What the tests change on the account

- Likes and saves the first feed post: TC-HOME-02 (likes, saves and opens comments on the first post). Undo by hand (or `HomePage.unlikePost()`).
- All login tests log in and out – many logins in a short time can trigger Instagram's security check.

## Account state expected

- Feed with at least one post and ≥ 5 stories (follow a few active accounts).
- Profile with 0 or more posts (both work).
- Notification permission granted or not – the popup is denied when it appears.
